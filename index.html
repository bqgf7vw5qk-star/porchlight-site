Porchlight

How was your day? Answer with the weather.

Porchlight is a tiny journal for iPhone. Each evening, pick the weather that matches your day, add a line, a few photos, and the people you were with. Over time, it becomes a quiet record of your everyday life and your family's small moments.

One tap a dayLog today's weather right from a reminder.
Your data stays yoursNo account, no ads, no tracking. Stored on your iPhone and your own iCloud.
Export anytimeJSON, Markdown, and CSV. Your journal is never locked in.
